# Terms of Use

By using Sentinel-Guard you agree to the following. These terms exist so the tool
stays on the safe side of the law internationally.

## Authorized use only
- You may only target hosts and networks that **you own or are explicitly authorized
  (in writing) to test**.
- You must declare those assets in a **signed scope** (`config/scope.yaml`). The tool
  refuses everything else.

## Prohibited
- Targeting third-party systems without authorization.
- "Hacking back" / retaliating against an attacker beyond blocking inbound traffic.
- Running attack/exploit validation against production (sandbox replicas only).
- Removing, disabling, or circumventing the safeguards (guard, consent, scope, audit,
  kill-switch), or redistributing a modified build with the safeguards weakened.

## Compliance
You are responsible for complying with all applicable laws, including but not limited to:
- Japan: Unauthorized Computer Access Act; Penal Code Arts. 168-2/168-3 (unauthorized
  command electromagnetic records, i.e. malware) and Art. 234-2 (obstruction of business
  by damaging a computer)
- US: Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA)
- UK: Computer Misuse Act
- Germany: StGB §202c
- Council of Europe Convention on Cybercrime (Budapest Convention) and local equivalents.
